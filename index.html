I am a systems researcher and a recent graduate of Columbia University, where I earned a B.S. in Computer Engineering magna cum laude. At Columbia's Software Systems Lab, I work on operating systems and memory management, most recently on bpf_fault, an eBPF-based framework for custom page fault handling, which won the Best Paper Award at SOSP 2026.

Previously, I built measurement tools and data pipelines for 6G wireless networks with Nokia Bell Labs and AT&T at Columbia's Wireless and Mobile Networking Lab, and interned as a software engineer at MongoDB and Northwestern Mutual. I am applying to PhD programs in computer systems.
